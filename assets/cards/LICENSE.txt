SVGCards — public-domain playing cards

Source: https://github.com/saulspatz/SVGCards
Commit: f8df28774736ea2545fc8e7fb693eb55ce031945
Files: Decks/Vertical2/svgs (52 faces, black/red jokers and blue back).

Saul Spatz places the scripts and graphics in the public domain. The source
README states: "Everything in this repository has been placed by the author
in the public domain."

The source credits the court illustrations to Byron Knoll and the back
designs to Openclipart. See the upstream README for its original credits and
links to Wikimedia Commons.

Rami Salon adaptations: the blue back is navy; number-card inner frames are
removed; the SVG illustrations have
been rasterized with Inkscape and packed into a shared texture with gutters.
The figures and pip arrangements are from the retrieved deck, not generated.

Rebuild: python3 scripts/build-card-atlas.py /path/to/SVGCards
Requires Inkscape and Pillow. Checkout the exact source commit above first.
